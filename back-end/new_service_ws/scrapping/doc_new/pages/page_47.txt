<h3>
                Lokasi Kampus Unpad            </h3>
<p>Unpad memiliki empat kampus strategis di beberapa lokasi, yaitu Kampus Dipati Ukur, Kampus Jatinangor (Utama), Kampus PSDKU Pangandaran, dan Kampus Kelas Jakarta. Keempat kampus ini menyediakan fasilitas pembelajaran, laboratorium, perpustakaan, serta co-working space yang nyaman sehingga membuat mahasiswa Unpad bahagia beraktivitas di lingkungan kampus.</p>
<h3>Kampus Bandung</h3>
<p>Kampus yang terletak di Kota Bandung menjadi pusat awal pendirian Unpad pada tahun 1957. Kampus Iwa Koesoemasoemantri ini menjadi simbol penting perjalanan perubahan transformasional Unpad.</p>

<h3>Kampus Jatinangor</h3>
<p>Merupakan pusat pengembangan karakter, akademik dan inovasi yang berada di lingkungan luas dan asri mencakup fakultas, laboratorium, perpustakaan, asrama mahasiswa, fasilitas olahraga dan kesehatan.</p>
<h3>Kampus Pangandaran</h3>
<p>Unpad berkomitmen pada pemerataan pendidikan di Jawa Barat melalui Kampus PSDKU Pangandaran. Pengalaman belajar bernuansa alam dan pantai, serta gedung akademik modern mendukung kreativitas dan inovasi.</p>
<h3>Kampus Jakarta</h3>
<p>Kampus perwakilan Jakarta merelevansikan akademik berkualitas dunia kerja dan perkembangan global. Pendekatan Hybrid Project Learning diterapkan program Magister bagi para profesional, karyawan dan entrepreneur.</p>

<h2>Studi di Indonesia <br/>Bersama <span>Unpad</span></h2>
<h5>Akash Rokade<br/>Faculty of Pharmacy in Unpad</h5>
<p>Erasmus+ Student from Italy</p>
<h3>“Diversity and Inclusivity”</h3>
<p>I am an Erasmus+ student from Italy, currently studying at the Faculty of Pharmacy at Unpad, Indonesia for one year. This experience has been both exciting and challenging, as I adapt to a new culture, language, and academic system. Being in Indonesia has broadened my perspective on healthcare and pharmaceutical practices, and I’ve had the opportunity to learn from diverse peers and professors. The Erasmus program has provided me with a unique opportunity to grow academically and personally, and I am grateful for the opportunity to connect with students from around the world while contributing to the academic community here.</p>
<h5>Fatuma Bwanaheri<br/>Master Program, International Relations</h5>
<p></p>
<p>I would like to express my sincere gratitude to the whole UNPAD fraternity, especially the FISIP and the international office, for your unwavering support. The administration and the Indonesian Aid Scholarship program coordinators in campus have been instrumental in ensuring a smooth academic journey. The lecturers have also been exceptionally dedicated to offering guidance, encouragement, and expertise that has been invaluable to my academic and personal growth. Thank you so much, UNPAD.
<br/><br/>— Awardee of the Indonesian Scholarship Awards (TIAS)</p>
<h5>Betelheme Melese<br/>International Undergraduate Program in International Relations</h5>
<p>I am from Ethiopia, and my name is Betelheme. I am an undergraduate student at the International Relations at Universitas Padjadjaran (UNPAD) right now. I have had nothing but support and generosity from this prestigious institution when I first came, which has made my time here genuinely remarkable. My knowledge of Indonesian culture and its diverse range of viewpoints has significantly increased as a result of my experience at UNPAD. I truly appreciate all of the chances I've had to get to know committed faculty members and other students. My experience has been much enhanced by these encounters, making it a noteworthy period of my life. I want to express my sincere gratitude to everyone at UNPAD for fostering such a welcoming and open environment.
<br/><br/>— Awardee of the Indonesian Scholarship Awards (TIAS)</p>
