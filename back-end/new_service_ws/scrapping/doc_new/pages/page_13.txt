<h2>Descriptions</h2>
<p><span>Universitas Padjadjaran offers an International Undergraduate Program (IUP) designed to provide Indonesian and international students with an opportunity to pursue undergraduate degrees in selected programs through uniquely designed learning methods that adhere to international standards.</span></p>
<p><span>Lectures are conducted in English, providing students with opportunities for international exposure, including student exchange at partner universities, international internships, and many more. Additionally, many programs offer double degree opportunities, enabling students to earn degrees from both Universitas Padjadjaran and a prestigious international partner university, enhancing global competitiveness and career prospects.</span></p>
<b>Available Programs</b>
<b>GLOBAL EXCELLENCE ACADEMY</b>
<li><span>English as Medium of Instruction (Courses are delivered fully in English)</span></li>
<li><span>Opportunities to obtain a double degree from a partner university.</span></li>
<li><span>Lectures from our overseas partner universities for certain courses.</span></li>
<li><span>Overseas internship programs or MNCs in Indonesia.</span></li>

<h2>Eligibility and Requirements</h2>
<p><span>Eligible Applicants:</span></p>
<li><span>Open to both Indonesian and International Applicants. (WNI and WNA)</span></li>
<li><span>Applicants must be high school graduates or equivalent from the academic years 2023, 2024, or 2025.</span></li>
<p><span>Application Process:</span></p>
<li><span>Complete the required information on the official admission page: </span><a><span>https://admission.unpad.ac.id/</span></a><span> </span></li>
<li><span>Refer to the detailed application procedures available on the website.</span></li>
<p><span>Proof of English Proficiency:</span></p>
<li><span>Submission of valid proof of English language proficiency is mandatory for all applicants, both Indonesian and international. Detailed requirements can be found in the application procedure.</span></li>
<li><span>International applicants from native English-speaking countries are exempted from the proof of English Proficiency, but instead should submit one page statement of purpose (motivation to study in Unpad, reason to select the study program, personal goals). </span></li>
<p><span>High School Academic Reports:</span></p>
<li><span>Indonesian applicants must provide their complete high school academic records from the first to fifth semesters.</span></li>
<li><span>International applicants are required to upload an original scan of their high school academic transcripts, from first to the latest semesters.</span></li>
<p><span>Interview for International Students:</span></p>
<li><span>International applicants will undergo an interview with the selection committee as part of the admission process.</span></li>

<h2>Admission Assessment </h2>
<p><span>The evaluation and admission of candidates will be based on the following criteria:</span></p>
<li><span>For Indonesian applicants: Submission of high school academic reports is mandatory and will form a significant part of the evaluation process.</span></li>
<li><span>For international applicants: Assessment will be based on an interview or academic transcripts.</span></li>
<li><span>Applicants must provide a valid score from a recognized English proficiency test. For details on approved test providers and minimum requirements, please refer to the Registration Procedure.</span></li>
<li><span>High school academic reports (mandatory for Indonesian applicants)</span></li>
<li><span>English proficiency test results</span></li>
<li><span>Under specific conditions, greater weight may be given to the academic report scores during the evaluation process.</span></li>
<li><span>All submitted documents and information will undergo a thorough verification process conducted by the admissions committee.</span></li>

<b>1. International Undergraduate Program in Accounting </b>
<p><span>The Accounting Bachelor’s degree program is an international undergraduate program in the Global Excellence Academy group. It has been internationally accredited by the Foundation for International Business Administration Accreditation (FIBAA) accreditation institution. As a study program in the Global Excellence Academy group, the learning process is entirely in English. Students have the opportunity for a double degree at several selected universities, namely Northern Illinois University (US) with a 2 + 2 scheme in semesters 5-8, the International Management Institute (IMI) Switzerland in semesters 6-7, the University of Northampton (UK) with a 3 + 1 scheme in semesters 7-8 and the University of Queensland (Australia) with a 3 + 1 scheme in semesters 6-7. Students can also exchange to partner universities abroad with lecturers from the designated partner universities. In study programs included in the Global Excellence Academy group, students will receive Global certification (AWS, Linkedin Learning, Google) during the learning period as well as a global case-based learning method.</span></p>
