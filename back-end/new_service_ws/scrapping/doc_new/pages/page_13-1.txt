Students in the Bachelor of Communication Science program will be awarded opportunities to partake in student exchanges at Sungkyunkwan University Korea, Myongji University Korea, Woosong University Korea, Ajou University Korea, and Hanyang University Korea for one or two semesters during the fifth or sixth semester of university.
